## 2. PROJECT CONCEPT
AstroCare is an aerospace biomedical intelligence platform intended to demonstrate how astronaut health and mission-related physiological and psychological risks can be monitored.

P11 focuses on:
ASTRONAUT WELLNESS, SLEEP, CIRCADIAN RHYTHM AND MENTAL HEALTH INTELLIGENCE.

Spaceflight can introduce multiple behavioural and physiological stressors, including:
- Sleep deprivation.
- Circadian rhythm disruption.
- Irregular schedules.
- Workload and fatigue.
- Isolation and confinement.
- Psychological adaptation challenges.
- Cognitive performance changes.
- Team communication and cohesion challenges.

NASA's Human Research Program investigates these challenges.
The P11 module should transform these research areas into a comprehensive astronaut wellness monitoring and analytics experience.

The application must clearly distinguish:
- Simulated astronaut measurements.
- User-entered demonstration records.
- Calculated analytical indicators.
- NASA research evidence.

Never present simulated records as actual NASA astronaut medical data.
